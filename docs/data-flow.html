What this graph does not show

Operators review logs and session evidence privately. This view contains no asset inventory, alert details, credentials, sensor addresses, or live paths. The public heartbeat describes a scheduled attempt, not the health of an individual service or relationship. A catalog entry or IP reputation score does not prove compromise.

Automatic blocking and VirusTotal voting are disabled. Shodan and BGP/ASN links are optional external references, not scheduled enrichment. XSOAR is a design reference, not an installed service.
